This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## 1. Information we collect
- **Account data:** your name, email, company name, and timezone.
- **Product data:** your match profiles (NAICS/PSC/keywords/agencies/etc.), saved
  opportunities, and notes.
- **Billing data:** subscription status and identifiers. **Payment card details are
  collected and stored by Stripe, not by us** — we never see full card numbers.
- **Technical data:** IP address, device/browser info, and standard server logs
  used for security and debugging.

We do **not** collect government opportunity data *about you*; opportunity records
come from the public SAM.gov feed and are not personal to you.

## 2. How we use it
To operate and improve the Service: match opportunities to your profiles, send your
daily digest and transactional emails (verification, password reset), provide
support, process billing, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

## 3. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. GDPR)
Performance of our contract with you (providing the Service), our legitimate
interests (security, product improvement), your consent (marketing, if any), and
legal obligations.

## 4. Sharing
We share data only with service providers ("processors") that help us run the
Service, under contract and only as needed:
- **Stripe** — payments and subscription management.
- **{{EMAIL_PROVIDER, e.g. Postmark}}** — transactional and digest email delivery.
- **{{HOSTING/DB provider, e.g. AWS}}** — hosting and database.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 5. Data retention
We keep account and product data while your account is active and for a reasonable
period afterward for legal, accounting, and security purposes, then delete or
anonymize it.

## 6. Your choices and rights
- Edit or delete your match profiles and saved items at any time in the app.
- Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing
  {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Depending on your location you may have additional rights
  (e.g. CCPA/GDPR); we honor applicable requests.
- Transactional emails (verification, billing) are required for the Service;
  product digests can be paused by deactivating your match profiles.

## 7. Security

refresh tokens, least-privilege access). No system is perfectly secure; we cannot
guarantee absolute security.
